Product and technical plan

Weekly Planning Notebook

A web application that turns a paper weekly planning spread into a fast planning workflow: weekly direction, weekly todos, day assignment, weekly notes, history, and a Sunday AI phone call that turns a short conversation into next week's plan.

Product Shape Two-page weekly spread: days on the left, to-do list and notes on the right
Signature Feature Sunday AI planning call that drafts next week automatically
Stack Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Better Auth, Drizzle, PostgreSQL
Deployment Vercel with Neon PostgreSQL and Vercel Cron

North Star

The app should feel like opening a weekly notebook, not managing a generic productivity database.

Core Promise

Help users set weekly direction, distribute work across days, and keep notes for the week, with a Sunday call that turns a five-minute conversation into next week's plan.

Primary Differentiator

A voice-first Sunday planning ritual on top of a weekly spread, task outcome tracking, weekly notes, and history. Planning happens by talking, not typing.

Design Constraint

The planner must be faster than paper for common actions. No heavy drawing editor in V1. The AI drafts, the user stays in control: every call-created item is visible, editable, and undoable.

Scope

V1 focuses on the planner and the Sunday AI call. Encryption and advanced visual note-taking are intentionally postponed.

V1 Includes

  • Social login and email/password login.
  • Weekly plan with ISO week number and date range.
  • Big picture direction for the week.
  • Weekly todo list.
  • Assign weekly tasks to specific days.
  • Add ad hoc daily tasks.
  • Mark tasks as completed or skipped.
  • Skipped reason visible in the UI.
  • One notes page per week: scrolls in place, saves while typing, full-screen mode.
  • Calendar/history for previous weeks.
  • Sunday AI planning call at a user-chosen day, time, and timezone.
  • Call result written automatically into next week: direction, tasks, day assignments, and notes.
  • Phone number verification, explicit call consent, and a "call me now" test call.
  • Email reminder as an alternative channel and as a fallback when a call is missed.
  • Call history with status, duration, and a short summary.

V1 Excludes

  • End-to-end encryption. Planned later as an optional mode; enabling it disables the AI call.
  • Inbound calls, mid-week check-in calls, and in-app voice planning.
  • Free drawing.
  • Boxes, arrows, dotted lines, and movable canvas objects.
  • Native mobile apps.
  • Complex rich-text editor features beyond basic formatting.
  • Analytics-heavy productivity scoring.

Weekly Planning

On Sunday the AI calls, recaps unfinished work, and asks about next week. After the call, next week's direction, tasks, and day assignments are already in the planner, marked as coming from the call.

Daily Use

Each day shows date, weekday, assigned tasks, ad hoc tasks, and a simple notes area. The default interaction should be quick entry.

Reflection

Previous weeks show completed work, skipped tasks with reasons, notes, and direction. This is history, not analytics-heavy performance scoring.

Sunday AI Planning Call

At the user's chosen time, an AI calls, talks through next week in plain language, and writes the result into the notebook. A managed voice platform runs the live call; the app owns scheduling, context, extraction, and writing the plan.

Conversation Outline

  1. Greet by name, say it is an AI assistant, confirm it is a good time.
  2. Recap unfinished and skipped tasks from this week and ask what carries over.
  3. Ask for the big picture direction for next week.
  4. Collect tasks in free form; ask for a day only when it matters.
  5. Ask for anything else to remember, such as appointments or notes.
  6. Read back a short summary and accept corrections.
  7. Say the plan will be waiting in the app and end the call.

Target 3 to 7 minutes, hard limit 10 minutes.

Plan Extraction

After the call, the backend sends the transcript plus this week's context to Claude (claude-opus-5-5) through the Anthropic TypeScript SDK with structured outputs, using a Zod schema:

  • bigPictureDirection
  • tasks[] with title, optional day, optional carriedOverFromTaskId
  • dayItems[] with day and text, added as tasks on that day
  • weekNotes, appended to the week's notes
  • callSummary

Day names resolve to dates in the target week: the ISO week that starts on the Monday after the call.

Applying The Plan

  • Validate the extraction with Zod before writing anything.
  • Append only. Never overwrite or delete user-entered items.
  • Fill direction only if next week's direction is empty; otherwise add the call's version as a note.
  • Tag every created task with call_id and show a "Drafted from your Sunday call" banner.
  • One-click undo removes everything that call created.
  • Processing is idempotent per call, so webhook retries cannot duplicate tasks.

Call Lifecycle

Status moves through queued, ringing, in_progress, ended, processed, with no_answer and failed as exits.

  • No answer or voicemail: hang up, retry once after 15 minutes.
  • Second miss: send the email reminder if email fallback is on.
  • Ended but not processed: the next cron run retries extraction.
  • Respect quiet hours and a per-user monthly call cap.

Consent And Compliance

  • Phone verified by SMS one-time code before any call.
  • Explicit opt-in with stored timestamp and consent text version.
  • The AI discloses that it is an AI at the start of every call.
  • "Stop calling me" on the call or in settings disables calls immediately.
  • Only call verified numbers, in an allowlist of countries.
  • Rules for automated calls differ by country, such as TCPA in the US and GDPR in the EU; review before launch.

Cost

Managed voice platforms land around $0.10 to $0.30 per minute all-in, including speech, voice, model, and telephony. A 5-minute weekly call is roughly $2 to $6 per user per month, more for non-US numbers.

Calls need a paid plan or a limited free allowance. Store cost per call to track margins.

Constraints And Concurrency

  • Unique weeks(user_id, iso_year, iso_week).
  • Direction and notes have separate versions, so editing one never conflicts with the other.
  • Unique planning_schedules(user_id) and calls(provider_call_id).
  • Partial index on planning_schedules(next_run_at) where enabled.
  • A version column plus If-Match on updates detects edits from two tabs or devices.

Week And Time Rules

  • Weeks are Monday-start ISO weeks.
  • iso_year is the ISO week-year: Dec 29 2025 belongs to 2026-W01.
  • Schedules store local day, local time, and IANA timezone.
  • next_run_at is recomputed after each run and whenever timezone or schedule changes, which also handles daylight saving time.
  • Weeks are created lazily on first write.

Ready For Optional Encryption

Keep content fields (title, body, big_picture_direction, skipped_reason) behind the planner module only. A later encrypted mode can then swap them for ciphertext per user without touching schedules, calls, or auth.

API Surface

Routes use RESTful nouns: the HTTP method decides the action, the path identifies the resource, and route paths avoid verbs. The user always comes from the session; user ids never appear in paths.

Module Example Endpoints Purpose
auth /api/auth/* Email/password and social sign-in, sessions, and sign-out. Routes are owned by Better Auth.
users GET /api/v1/me, PATCH /api/v1/me, DELETE /api/v1/me Profile, timezone, and account deletion.
weeks GET /api/v1/years/:isoYear/weeks, GET /api/v1/years/:isoYear/weeks/:isoWeek, PUT /api/v1/years/:isoYear/weeks/:isoWeek List weeks for history, read one week with its tasks and notes, set the big picture direction.
tasks POST /api/v1/years/:isoYear/weeks/:isoWeek/tasks, PATCH /api/v1/tasks/:taskId, DELETE /api/v1/tasks/:taskId Create tasks, assign them to days, reorder, and mark done or skipped with a reason.
notes PUT /api/v1/years/:isoYear/weeks/:isoWeek/notes Replace the week's notes. If-Match carries the notes version.
planning-schedule GET /api/v1/me/planning-schedule, PUT /api/v1/me/planning-schedule Channel, day, time, timezone, enabled state, email fallback, and call consent.
phone-verifications POST /api/v1/me/phone-verifications, PATCH /api/v1/me/phone-verifications/:verificationId Send an SMS code, then submit the code to verify the number. Rate limited.
calls GET /api/v1/calls, GET /api/v1/calls/:callId, POST /api/v1/calls, DELETE /api/v1/calls/:callId/plan-import Call history and summaries, start a test call now, and undo everything a call added.
webhooks POST /api/webhooks/voice Signature-verified call events from the voice platform. Stores the event and returns quickly.
cron GET /api/cron/planning-runs Internal only, protected by CRON_SECRET. Vercel Cron always calls with GET.

Privacy And Data Handling

Planner content is readable by the server in V1 so the AI call can use it. Privacy comes from minimization, access control, short retention, and clear disclosure.

V1 rule: planner content never appears in emails, SMS, logs, error reports, or analytics. Only the user, the call pipeline, and audited production access can read it.
Data Where It Goes Retention Notes
Login password Better Auth Hash only Plain passwords are never stored or logged.
Planner content Neon PostgreSQL, encrypted at rest by the provider Until the user deletes it or the account Direction, tasks, notes, and skipped reasons.
Call context Voice platform and Claude, per call Per call only First name, this week's direction, and unfinished task titles. Nothing older.
Phone number PostgreSQL, voice platform, Twilio Verify Until removed by the user Stored in E.164 format, verified before use.
Call audio Not recorded None Recording is turned off on the voice platform.
Transcript Voice platform, then our database and Claude 30 days in our database, deleted at the provider after processing Lets the user see what the AI heard and helps debug extraction.

Sub-processors

Vercel, Neon, the voice platform and its telephony carrier, Anthropic, Twilio, and Resend. List them in the privacy policy, sign data processing agreements, prefer EU regions where offered, and use each provider's lowest data retention settings.

Later: Optional End-to-End Encryption

A user can turn on an encrypted mode: vault passphrase, recovery key, and client-side AES-GCM. The server can then no longer read or write planner content, so enabling it turns off the AI call for that user, encrypts existing weeks in the browser, and deletes stored transcripts. Schedules and email reminders keep working.

Vercel Setup

  • Pro plan: Hobby cron runs only once a day with up to 59 minutes of delay.
  • Fluid compute; raise maxDuration on webhook and extraction routes.
  • Pin the function region next to the Neon database region.
  • Preview deployments use Neon branch databases.
  • Run Drizzle migrations in CI before promoting to production.
  • Live call audio stays on the voice platform, not on Vercel functions.

Cron Run

  1. Claim due schedules with one UPDATE ... RETURNING so overlapping runs cannot double-dial.
  2. Start calls or send email reminders.
  3. Retry unanswered calls once.
  4. Re-run extraction for calls that ended but were not processed.
  5. Delete expired transcripts.

Roadmap

Build the planner first, then the call on top of it, so the call always has somewhere useful to write.

Foundation

Next.js App Router project with strict TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Drizzle with Neon, Vercel project with preview deployments, CI for lint, typecheck, and tests, and a health check.

Authentication

Better Auth with email/password and social login, database sessions in secure HTTP-only cookies, protected routes, profile, and timezone.

Planner MVP

Current week view, ISO week notation, weekly direction, weekly todos, daily assignment, ad hoc daily tasks, notes, task completion, skipped reasons, and optimistic updates.

Calendar And Email Reminders

Previous week navigation, calendar history, planning schedule with timezone handling, Vercel Cron, and email reminders through Resend.

Sunday AI Call

Phone verification, consent, voice platform adapter, conversation script, signed webhooks, Claude extraction, merge into next week, undo, retries and email fallback, call history, cost tracking, and the test call.

Polish And Launch

Responsive layout, accessibility checks, onboarding with a first test call, privacy policy with sub-processors, rate limits, backups through Neon point-in-time restore, error monitoring, and beta launch.

Later

Optional end-to-end encryption mode that disables the AI call, in-app voice planning, mid-week check-in calls, and native mobile apps.

Risks And Decisions

The hard parts are call quality, call cost, consent, and keeping the planner fast.

Key Risks

  • The AI mishears or misplaces tasks. Mitigate with a read-back on the call, tagged items, and one-click undo.
  • Call cost scales per user. Needs pricing, a free allowance, and per-user caps.
  • Users miss Sunday calls. One retry, then an email reminder.
  • Automated AI calls need explicit consent, AI disclosure, and easy opt-out, with rules that differ by country.
  • SMS and call toll fraud through phone verification. Rate limit and restrict countries.
  • Planner content is readable server-side in V1. Disclose it and keep it out of logs and emails.
  • Dependence on one voice platform. Keep it behind a thin adapter.
  • Timezone and daylight saving bugs can call users at the wrong time.
  • Too much rich text or drawing scope can delay the planner MVP.

Decisions Locked For V1

  • Next.js App Router with TypeScript, deployed on Vercel.
  • shadcn/ui and Tailwind for the UI.
  • Better Auth with database sessions in secure HTTP-only cookies.
  • Drizzle ORM on Neon PostgreSQL.
  • Normalized planner tables, no encryption in V1.
  • Sunday AI call through Vapi behind a voice adapter; Claude for post-call extraction.
  • Local development runs on PGlite with a simulated call, so no services are needed.
  • Call results apply automatically, tagged and undoable.
  • Email reminder as an alternative channel and fallback.
  • Simple text notes in V1.
  • Modular monolith with server-only modules.
  • Encryption comes later as an optional mode that disables the AI call.

Open Technical Decisions

  1. Voice platform V1 ships with Vapi behind a thin adapter. Before launch, compare outbound rates to your launch countries with Retell and ElevenLabs Agents; switching means writing one adapter.
  2. Conversation model and latency Vapi only accepts its own list of Anthropic models, which does not include Claude Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5. V1 uses claude-sonnet-5 for the live call (configurable with VOICE_MODEL); extraction runs on claude-opus-5-5 through the Anthropic API. Measure turn latency on real calls.
  3. Transcript retention Keep transcripts 30 days so users can check what the AI heard, or delete them right after extraction.
  4. Pricing for calls Paid tier only, or a small number of free calls per month before upgrading.
  5. Basic note formatting Start plain text. Add bold or color only if it does not complicate editing or mobile ergonomics.
